### Fixed
- **A storage panic during session refresh no longer costs the caller's login budget.** `POST /auth/refresh` used to answer a storage panic with a 500 and keep the per-IP login-attempt slot, even when the panic hit the session lookup, before any token was checked. Each storage call in the refresh now handles a panic exactly like its error: a lookup panic hands the slot back and answers 503, and a panic after the token matched keeps the slot (503 for the owner read, the wrong-token 401 for a write). A panic while listing the session family on a replayed token no longer skips the family revocation (#3968).
- **`POST /api/v1/user-roles` answers a grant-ceiling refusal 403, not 500.** Assigning a role whose permissions the actor does not hold now gets 403 with core's message, with or without `expires_at`, the same answer group and machine role assignment give (#3979).

### Testing
- **Derived guard over every handler that can hit the grant ceiling.** `internal/core` derives which exported methods can refuse with the grant ceiling (`testdata/grant_ceiling_reaching_methods.txt`), and `TestEveryGrantingHandlerMapsTheCeilingRefusal` requires every HTTP handler calling one to classify `core.ErrPermissionDenied`. Four handlers that still answer it 500 are exempted naming #4154.
