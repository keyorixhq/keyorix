### Changed
- **A member can list their own break-glass activations.** `GET /api/v1/projects/{id}/break-glass`, `BreakGlassService.ListBreakGlassActivations` and `keyorix break-glass list` used to require `roles.read`, so a member who had just activated emergency access could not see it. A caller with `roles.read` at the project still gets every activation (`"scope": "project"`, unchanged); any other current member of the project now gets only their own activations (active, expired and revoked, same fields, `"scope": "own"`), and nothing about anyone else's, not even a count. A non-member (the same 403 for every project id, so no project existence or MFA setting leaks), a machine identity without `roles.read`, a restricted PAT whose restriction does not allow `roles.read` at the project, or a failed authorization read are refused with 403 / PermissionDenied as before. The per-project MFA policy applies to both views; listing is still not audited. The CLI labels the own view ("Your break-glass activations"). Guarded by `server/http/break_glass_own_list_test.go`, its gRPC and core twins, and journey 9 (INV-CORE-break-glass-list-own-only).
