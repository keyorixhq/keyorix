### Testing
- **Stateful authz sequence fuzzing.** `server/authzdiff` gains `FuzzAuthzSeq` and an opt-in sweep (`AUTHZSEQ_SWEEP=1`) that run random sequences of grants, revokes, group changes, suspensions and permission toggles on the core, REST and gRPC legs, and compare every step and every probe with the reference model. A transport allowing what the model denies, any state-effect (O-E) finding such as a revoke, suspension or group removal that left its row behind, and a step that ends in a 5xx or untyped error are never treated as single-step noise. The default `go test` runs the baseline, one benign sequence and one planted defect (`revoke-noop`); `AUTHZSEQ_SELFTEST=1` runs the rest. The reference model now treats a project-scoped group membership as conferring nothing at global scope, which matches the store's group-role resolution.
