### Testing
- **Hostile-server fuzzing of the pgx query path.** `FuzzPgxHostileQuery` drives pgx/v5 query, prepared, COPY and multi-result operations against a fake server whose post-handshake byte stream is the fuzz input. Oracles (no panic or hang, allocation budget, the admin password never in errors or decoded values) are pinned red/green, and a known-panic allowlist still fails on any new panic site. Layout and oracle limits: `fuzz/thirdparty/README.md`.
