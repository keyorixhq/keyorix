### Documentation
- **ADR-117 addendum (Proposed): signed-policy distribution, recovery authority and no fallback.** Design only, no behaviour change. It defines how a new signed PCR policy reaches a host and when the old one stops being accepted, who may authorise a TPM recovery and that the audit event is written before the blob is replaced. A policy-bound `tpm` provider must be the only provider in its chain, and `insecure_allow_weaker_kek_fallback` does not lift that. It also corrects ADR-117 D9: a DEK sweep alone does not cut off an old sealed blob; that takes a new KEK first.
