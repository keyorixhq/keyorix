### Testing
- **Egress guard wiring is now fuzzed per site.** Eight new test-only fuzz targets (`FuzzJWKSTransportEgressParity`, `FuzzOIDCDiscoveryTransportEgressParity`, `FuzzAzureGraphTransportEgressParity`, `FuzzConnect{Vault,Azure,AWS}TransportEgressParity`, `FuzzAzureKMSTransportEgressParity`, `FuzzAzureKMSResolverRebinding`) dial each guarded transport with one address in ten encodings (IPv4-mapped, NAT64, 6to4, Teredo, zone-suffixed link-local, metadata addresses) and compare against an independent reference in `internal/fuzzutil/egressref`. Each target dials through the client its site really builds (the OIDC discovery target, whose client is built inline, drives its transport variable), so a site that drops the guard turns its target red; `azureKVHTTPClient` and `awsSMHTTPClient` in `internal/connect` are the seams for that, with no behaviour change. The oracles are red-proofed by planted bypasses in that package's tests.
