### Fixed
- **A pilot SDK now sees the right status for the last text-matched error families.** `GET /api/v1/projects/{id}`, `GET /api/v1/groups/{id}`, `GET /api/v1/secret-templates/{id}` and `GET /api/v1/secrets/{id}/sharing-status` answered 404 whenever the error text contained "not found", so a storage read fault with those words read as a missing target, and a plain fault was a 500. Core now returns the existing not-found sentinel, the handler reads it with `errors.Is`, and a storage fault is the family's 503 `storage_unavailable` with `Retry-After`. A caller with no access to a secret's sharing status is a 403 by sentinel, not by the word "permission". `DELETE /auth/sessions/{id}` answers 404 only for a session that is missing or not the caller's; a failed delete is a 500 and an audit outage a 503 (it was a 404).
- **The grant ceiling is classified by sentinel.** `POST /api/v1/users` and `POST /api/v1/groups/{id}/members` read the refusal as `core.ErrPermissionDenied` instead of the words "cannot grant this role". A guard fails any handler that spells the phrase and any untyped `fmt.Errorf`/`errors.New` with that text in `internal/core`; a family test drives eight grant routes as an actor who lacks the granted role's permissions and requires 403 from each. `POST /api/v1/projects/{id}/machine-identities/{machineId}/roles` answered such a refusal 500; it is 403 now.
- **The machine-identity, OIDC-binding and dynamic-secret routes classify core's refusals by sentinel.** A read fault while resolving the environment or role of a machine role binding is 503 (it was 404 or 500); a missing environment or role is 404, an environment of another project 400, an already-assigned or not-assigned role and an already-bound OIDC subject 409. A dynamic-secret config or lease that is gone is 404 and a refused input 400 (both were 502).
- **OpenAPI.** Documents the 403 of `POST /api/v1/groups/{id}/roles`, the 503 of `POST /api/v1/users`, `POST /api/v1/invitations` and `POST /api/v1/rotation-policies`, and the rule behind the dashboard's `inactiveUsers` (last login, or creation time for a user who never logged in, more than 30 days ago). `web/src/types/api-generated.ts` is regenerated.
