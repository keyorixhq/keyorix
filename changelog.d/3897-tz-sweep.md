### Testing
- **Solewriter one-fenced-write tests wait for the store's startup writes before sampling.** `TestTracker_GrantWithItsAuditRowIsOneFencedWrite` and `TestTracker_SecretMutationWithItsAuditRowIsOneFencedWrite` took their tracker baseline while the trigger-integrity check's first tick was still running in the background; the tracker counts that catalog read as a fenced write, so on a fast runner the test saw one commit or one in-flight transaction too many (it flaked in the merge queue). The fixture now waits until the commit and in-flight counts have been still for 400 ms (bounded at 15 s, then fails) before the test samples them; the assertions are unchanged.
- **`scripts/ci/tz-sweep.sh`** runs time-sensitive Go packages one at a time under non-UTC zones (default Asia/Kolkata and America/Los_Angeles) and lists every failing zone/package pair; a zone Go cannot load is refused (exit 2). Not wired into CI.
