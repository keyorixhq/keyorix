### Security
- **Core refuses a secret owner who lacks the write/delete permission (#4160).** `UpdateSecretWithPermissionCheck` and `DeleteSecretWithPermissionCheck` no longer let ownership alone authorize the mutation: an owner whose roles grant neither `secrets.write` (update) nor `secrets.delete` (delete), and who holds no ACL or allowlisted share covering it, is refused with `ErrPermissionDenied`, the same decision the per-secret REST route and gRPC RPC gates already made. Defence in depth: no client could reach this, every transport refused it first. Guarded by `TestCoreOwnerWithoutPermissionIsRefused` (server/authzdiff); authzdiff waiver known-03 removed.
- **Core refuses mutations by a suspended or deprovisioned account (#4159).** Every mutating core entry point the authzdiff harness binds (role grants, project member add/role change, invitations and member invites, access-request approval, machine token issue and role assignment, break-glass activation, membership transitions, environment clone/copy, and the secret mutations: update, delete, rotate, rollback, auto-rotate, copy, move, tags, description, dependencies, extend-expiring, owner reassignment, share revoke, certificate inspection) now refuses a human actor whose account is not active with `ErrPermissionDenied`, through one helper (`requireActorActive`). The transports already refused such an account at authentication, so no client could reach this; it is defence in depth. Consequence: a pending invitation from an inviter who has since been suspended no longer grants on acceptance (the accept re-checks the inviter's authority). Guarded by `TestCoreRefusesSuspendedActor` (server/authzdiff); authzdiff waiver known-02 narrowed to the two value-read ops, which belong to the read path.
