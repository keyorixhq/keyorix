### Testing
- **Hostile-source harness: the self-loop redirect bound no longer counts independent requests as hops.** The `self-loop-307` program flagged a connector as having followed a redirect after 12 requests to one route, so `healthscan` (which makes 20+ different `/v1/sys/...` calls on one route) failed `FuzzHealthscanHostileVault` with a false `O9/redirect-followed`. Hops are now counted per route and URL. Both copies of the harness (`migrate/internal/hostile`, `internal/fuzzutil/hostile`) gain a planted-defect proof (a connector that loops on its own 307 is still caught) and a negative control (25 distinct calls are not).
