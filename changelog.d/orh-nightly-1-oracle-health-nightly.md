### Testing

- **Oracle health runs nightly on pve01, without a workflow.** `scripts/fuzzing/oracle-health/nightly/oracle-health-nightly.sh` (with a systemd service and timer and an EnvironmentFile example, no credentials) fast-forwards a dedicated clone to `origin/main`, runs `oracle-health.sh --heavy --strict` at nice 10 with `GOMAXPROCS=2` and tmpfs scratch, keeps a dated log and a one-line-per-entry summary, and sends one ntfy message (at most one per entry per day) when any verdict is not `DETECTED` or an accepted `KNOWN-BLIND`. It skips quietly while `PVE-BENCH-LOCK` exists or the host is hot or busy, and alerts after three skipped nights in a row. Guarded by `tests/oracle_health_nightly.bats` (stubbed runner, curl and gate); runbook in `docs/fuzzing/oracle-health-nightly.md`. Scripts and docs only; the units are not installed by this change.
