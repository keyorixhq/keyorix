### Fixed
- **`admin verify-audit --db` works on a WAL-mode SQLite copy in a read-only directory and leaves it untouched (#4350).** Every keyorix SQLite database is WAL-mode, and opening one in place, even read-only, created `-wal`/`-shm` beside the evidence copy and failed with SQLite error 1544 on a read-only directory. The verifier now copies the database and its `-wal` (if any) into a private `0700` directory under `$TMPDIR`, verifies the copy and deletes it afterwards, also on error. An un-checkpointed `-wal` is still read (no `immutable=1`). Not enough free space under `$TMPDIR` is a clear input error. Guarded by `TestOpenSQLiteReadOnly_WALFileInReadOnlyDirVerifies`, `_WALFileLeavesDirectoryUnchanged`, `_UncheckpointedWALRowIsVerified` and the `FuzzAuditVerifyHostileSQLiteFile` oracle, which no longer tolerates the sidecars.
