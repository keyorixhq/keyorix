- **INV-STORE-secret-purge-leaves-no-orphans** `PurgeDeletedSecretsBefore` leaves no row that references the purged secret or one of its versions, except the columns listed in `backupfmt.HistoricalReferences` (`audit_events.secret_node_id`, which is append-only, and the `access_review_items.secret_id` snapshot). Every other referencing column is deleted in the purge's own transaction (`SecretPurgeCascades`, `SecretPurgeIndirectCascades`); a failed cascade statement rolls the whole purge back. Why: #3085 — orphans made `admin restore` refuse a backup taken after an ordinary purge, and audit rows may never be deleted or rewritten. Guard: `internal/backupfmt/purge_refs_guard_test.go:TestSecretReferencesAreClassified` (a new referencing column must be classified), `local_purge_secret_refs_test.go:TestPurgeDeletedSecretsBefore_RemovesEveryClassifiedReference` and `:TestPurgeDeletedSecretsBefore_CascadeFailureRollsBackThePurge`, `server/admin/backup_restore_purge_test.go:TestAdminBackupRestore_AfterSecretPurge_SQLite` (and `_Postgres`).
